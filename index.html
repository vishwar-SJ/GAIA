Urgent Challenges Solved

Why GAIA Was Born in the Mathigiri Forests

After successive ecological and human-wildlife crises devastated the Mathigiri Reserve Forest and Cauvery Wildlife Sanctuary in Hosur, India, a dedicated team of student engineers engineered an autonomous IoT & Edge AI conservation shield.

🔥

Midnight Forest Wildfires

In January 2020, a midnight fire swept through Cauvery Wildlife Sanctuary. Due to a total lack of automated nocturnal detection, fire raged undetected for hours, ravaging indigenous flora and animal habitats before rangers could mobilize.

✓ Solved: Multi-spectral thermal, gas & AI camera nodes alert upon hazard detection.
🐆

Deadly Wildlife Intrusions

Severe human-animal conflicts peaked when a leopard entered rural village settlements, killing 23 domestic livestock animals (cattle, stray dogs, goats) and attacking 5 people. Meanwhile, herds of 37 elephants frequently decimate agricultural crops.

✓ Solved: PIR-activated camera traps (CT-Orion & CT-Sirius) track corridor movement.
🪵

Sandalwood & Timber Theft

Rampant illegal wood trafficking targeting prized sandalwood, teak, and aged timber threatened biodiversity. Poachers operated silently in dead zones beyond normal patrol perimeters without physical deterrence.

✓ Solved: 6-axis accelerometers and vibration sensors detect chainsaw & chopping tremors.
Edge Technology & Hardware

Integrated Multi-Tier Sensing Architecture

GAIA combines subterranean soil monitoring, high-canopy atmospheric nodes, embedded Sony IMX AI vision sensors, and high-gain LoRaWAN infrastructure into an impenetrable forest monitoring network.

📡

Dual-Elevation Sensor Nodes

Engineered with modular RAK WisBlock modules across two critical vantage points:

  • Tree Canopy Tier: BME680 gas/temp/humidity/pressure, ambient light, flame sensors.
  • Ground Subterranean Tier: Soil moisture, soil temperature, precipitation, and seismic disturbance sensors.
🧠

Edge AI Camera Traps (Sony IMX + Pi)

High-efficiency edge machine learning running on Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and Sony IMX AI Camera modules:

  • 4-Class Inference: Real-time detection of Fire Only, Smoke Only, Combined Fire/Smoke, and Normal.
  • Zero Cloud Latency: Models converted to native Sony IMX binary format for ultra-low power execution.
📶

Long-Range LoRaWAN Gateway

Central RAK Gateway operating in the IN865 band across 16 frequency channels equipped with dual 5.8 dBi high-gain antennas.

  • Maintains robust 15 km telemetry coverage across mountainous, dense canopy terrain.
  • Bridges payloads over Airtel 4G LTE to The Things Stack cloud server.
🔋

Custom Battery Management System (BMS)

High-safety, thermal-stable energy design engineered for harsh remote woodland conditions:

  • 2S LiFePO4 (6V, 6Ah 32650 cells) with MP1584 buck converters and slow-blow fuse protection.
  • Multi-year Saft Li-SOCl2 (3.6V, 2.6Ah) primary cells for autonomous RAK nodes.
