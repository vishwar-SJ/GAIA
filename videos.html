Key Video Insights

What the Demonstrations Showcase

1. Rapid Edge AI Inference

The Sony IMX AI camera captures frames every 15 minutes or when awakened by PIR motion sensors, running on-device PyTorch-converted binary models without requiring high-bandwidth video uplinks.

2. 8 km Rugged LoRa Transmission

Watch the gateway receiver process packet telemetry in real-time across dense foliage and severe elevation changes between the Anchetty valley and high ridge towers.

3. Extreme Battery Autonomy

Observe how the custom 2S LiFePO4 battery pack and MP1584 buck converters maintain a steady 5.1V rail, smoothly powering the Raspberry Pi through active and deep-sleep states.
