Meet the GAIA Engineering Team

Developed by student innovators from the IEEE Student Branch of Sri Sai Ram Engineering College, Chennai, dedicated to advancing conservation technology in wild forest frontiers.

Community & Volunteer Force

Deploying sensitive electronic equipment deep inside protected forests demands physical resilience and community trust. Alongside the student engineering branch, our 4 field volunteers and local naturalists traversed steep terrain to mount canopy nodes, anchor solar mast towers, and validate GPS tracking.

Institutional Ecosystem

Partner Organizations & Mentors

GAIA's success is rooted in strong multi-stakeholder collaboration spanning academia, global engineering standards, non-profit wildlife trusts, and state forest authorities.

🏛️

Sri Sai Ram Engineering College, Chennai

Student Branch (Autonomous • Affiliated with Anna University)

Provided state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, faculty mentorship, prototyping equipment, and logistical encouragement enabling students to translate theoretical electronics into robust field systems.

🌐

IEEE Standards Association & EPICS in IEEE

International Funding Partner ($6,429 Grant)

Engineering Projects in Community Service (EPICS in IEEE) funded the procurement of specialized WisBlock sensors, Sony IMX AI cameras, LoRaWAN gateways, and high-capacity battery packs.

🌿

Kenneth Anderson Nature Society (KANS)

Conservation NGO & Wildlife Field Partner

Provided domain expertise on elephant migratory corridors, leopard territory mapping in Hosur, server infrastructure for web telemetry, and ecological guidance to ensure minimal animal disruption.

🛡️

Tamil Nadu Forest Department

Cauvery North Wildlife Sanctuary • Hosur Division

Granted field testing permits in the Anchetty Reserve Forest, accompanied student teams with forest rangers, provided terrain elevation insights, and participated in live alarm verification.
